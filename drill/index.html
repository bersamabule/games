Expedition to planet 1.7305.d

DRILL

Your satellite was wrecked over planet 1.7305.d and only the drill ship survived. Your main objective: rebuild the satellite by selling or buying 100 of every ore (coal, copper, iron and sulfur) and 100 of every bar (copper, iron and sulfur) at the depot. Dig the ore out of the rock. Drilling to the center, 800 m down, is a side goal. The ship’s smelter turns ore into bars while you drill, and bars sell for far more at the depot. The ship has no fuel tank, so the drill and thrusters never run dry.

This screen, and sound
  • Objective. The satellite is rebuilt once you have sold or bought 100 of each of the 7 items. Ore is sold with the “Sell raw” button at the depot. Bars are sold automatically when you dock, and bars cast while you are parked there sell straight away.
  • Crash site. Your ship came down with the wreck of the satellite. Drive over all 6 pieces of debris on the surface to build the depot from them.
  • Shop. The Shop tab at the depot sells coal, copper, iron, sulfur and all 3 bars, at twice the price it pays for them. Bought goods count toward the satellite as soon as you buy them, and they go into the hold, so you can also buy the coal a recipe is missing. Selling them again only gets you cash.
  • Smelting. 2 copper + 1 coal makes a copper bar, 2 iron + 1 coal makes an iron bar, and 2 sulfur makes a sulfur bar.
  • Two limits on the way down. Your bit cuts rock up to its Mohs hardness, and your heat shield handles layers up to its temperature. The core log shows which layers are still out of reach.
  • Hazards. Violet blocks are gas pockets, and they explode when you drill into them. Magma, liquid iron and drops of more than 8 m dent the hull.
