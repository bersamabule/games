Install it like an app

Open a game first, then follow the steps for your device. Each game gets its own icon, plays full screen, and works with no internet.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open a game.
  2. Tap the three dots menu.
  3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.

iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Open a game in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Windows / Mac (Chrome or Edge)

  1. Open a game.
  2. Click the Install icon at the right end of the address bar.
  3. Click Install.

Your progress is saved on your own device.
