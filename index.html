Install it like an app

Put Drill Games on your phone

Install this page and you get one icon. Tap it, then tap Show QR, and a friend can scan it and play right away.

Use the steps below, but open this page instead of a game.

Or install one game on its own. Open a game first, then follow the steps for your device. Each game gets its own icon, plays full screen, and works with no internet.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open a game.
  2. Tap the three dots menu.
  3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.

iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Open a game in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Windows / Mac (Chrome or Edge)

  1. Open a game.
  2. Click the Install icon at the right end of the address bar.
  3. Click Install.

Your progress is saved on your own device.
