Robot attack on Novaport

DRILL 3

The Meridian made it home from planet 173v4, and something followed it out of the hollow under Halden Station. Robot drop pods are crashing into Novaport, and the robots are tearing the city apart. Drive the drill ship into every robot to break it, but if a robot drives into you first, it is game over. When the last robot is down, mine coal, copper, iron and sulfur under the streets, smelt bars, and rebuild the city.

Help: this screen. The game waits while it is up
  • Who hits first wins. Drive or drill into a robot to break it. Just before a robot charges, it crouches and its eyes flare white. Hit it first, or get out of the way.
  • The robots. Scouts climb walls and chase you. Diggers tunnel through rock to reach you. Wreckers have 2 armor plates and the Titan has 6, so they take several hits, and each hit knocks them back.
  • Pit traps. Wreckers and the Titan cannot climb. Dig a hole in the street and they fall in.
  • The works yard. Robots cannot get through its blast gate. Park on the pad to patch the hull, sell ore and scrap, and buy upgrades. Ram plating breaks more armor with each hit.
  • Rebuilding. Once all 3 waves are down, park in front of a damaged building to fit the bars you carry: iron bars for steel beams, copper bars for wiring and sulfur bars for sulfur concrete. The power station also burns coal.
  • Smelting. 2 copper + 1 coal makes a copper bar, 2 iron + 1 coal makes an iron bar, and 2 sulfur makes a sulfur bar. Broken robots also drop scrap bars.
  • Hazards. Violet blocks are gas pockets and explode, and a robot that digs into one blows up too. Granite below 130 m needs a carbide bit.
