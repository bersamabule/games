    DRILL 2

    The satellite over 1.7305.d is rebuilt. Your starship, the Meridian, was crossing the galaxy toward home when it picked up a distress call. It comes from Halden Station, a research outpost that sank deep under the surface of planet 173v4. You came down in an escape pod with the drill ship. Follow the signal underground, find out what happened, rescue whoever is still down there, and repair the pod so you can all launch back to the Meridian.

    This screen
    • The signal. The arrow next to Signal at the top points to the source, and the strength rises as you get closer. The pings speed up too. After the rescue the arrow points back to the pod.
    • Flight recorders. Halden’s 5 recorders fell down the collapsed shaft. Their orange lights blink in the rock. Drill into one to play its log.
    • The pod. Park on the glassy landing pad to use the pod. Bars go into the launch engine repairs first, and the rest are sold. The Shop tab sells ore, and bars that go straight into the repairs.
    • Smelting. 2 copper + 1 coal makes a copper bar, 2 iron + 1 coal makes an iron bar, and 2 sulfur makes a sulfur bar.
    • Two limits on the way down. Your bit cuts rock up to its Mohs hardness, and your heat shield handles layers up to its temperature. The core log shows which layers are still out of reach.
    • Hazards. Violet blocks are gas pockets and explode. Magma and drops of more than 8 m dent the hull.
    • Backups. Your mission saves itself in this browser. To keep a copy somewhere else, or to carry on with another browser or device, press 💾 Backups on this screen: save a file or copy a save code, then load it on the other one.
